Methodology · How This Stays Honest

01 · Universe Fetched at Runtime

On every page load, the dashboard fetches the current S&P 500 constituent list from a public open-data source (datasets/s-and-p-500-companies on GitHub). Nothing is hardcoded. If a company joins or leaves the index, this dashboard reflects it on the next reload.

02 · Stratified Random Sampling

Each scan selects N stocks via round-robin across 11 GICS sectors with random ordering inside each sector. Tech cannot dominate the sample just because the index is tech-heavy. Already-scored stocks are skipped until the cache expires.

03 · Five-Factor Composite

Momentum 25% (52w range position, 13/26/52w returns) · Quality 30% (net margin, ROE, ROA, current ratio) · Value 15% (PE, PB, PS) · Growth 20% (5y revenue and EPS CAGR, TTM growth) · Sentiment 10% (analyst recommendation distribution).

04 · Quantitative Moat Detection

Moat tag is computed from raw fundamentals, not assigned by hand. Wide moat = gross margin >40%, ROE >15%, and net margin >15% (all three). Narrow = any two of three. None detected = zero or one. Imperfect proxy, but transparent and applied uniformly across all 500+ stocks.

05 · Coverage Compounds Over Time

Each scan accumulates into the scored pool. Click "Score More" repeatedly to grow coverage. With default sample size of 25, full S&P 500 coverage takes about 20 scans. Scores are cached for 24 hours, then refresh on next scan.

06 · Top 3 = Best of Pool

Top 3 picks are the three highest composite scores in the entire scored pool, not just the latest sample. As coverage grows, picks become more representative of the full index. Picks shift naturally as new stocks score higher than incumbents.

The S&P 500 is itself a curation. The S&P committee selects constituents by market cap, profitability, liquidity, and US domicile. Small caps, micro caps, and most international names are absent from this universe. If you want broader coverage, you would need a paid API tier (Polygon, Financial Modeling Prep, or Tiingo) with bulk endpoints. Within the S&P 500 universe, this dashboard applies no further selection bias.

This is not financial advice. The composite is a deterministic ranking on observable metrics. It cannot account for regime shifts, fraud risk, regulatory changes, technology disruption, or qualitative factors. Verify every data point before any investment decision.
